About

Dr. Hadar Cohen Duwek is a researcher and lecturer in computational neuroscience, studying how the brain represents and learns from sensory information — and how those principles can be translated into efficient, brain-inspired computing.

Her work spans spiking neural networks, neuromorphic hardware, and models of perception, combining theory, simulation, and close attention to biological plausibility. She teaches and mentors students at the Open University of Israel, where she leads research bridging neuroscience and machine learning.
